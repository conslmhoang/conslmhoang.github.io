Civil engineer, graduate researcher in construction materials

I came to concrete from the ground up. My first research used finite element models and neural networks to judge how well cement deep mixing (CDM) improves soft soil. Now, at the Construction Materials Lab of Chonnam National University, I build machine-learning models that predict the compressive and tensile behaviour of fiber-reinforced cementitious composites from their mix design.

Projects

Construction Materials Lab: mix design & strength prediction

JavaScript, Python, machine learning

A browser tool for ECC/SHCC, UHPC and geopolymer composites. It turns mix proportions into batch quantities with cost and CO₂, and predicts compressive strength and tensile strain capacity from the mix, fibers and curing, with an 80% range.

Open the app Source on GitHub

HDC Wiki: high-ductility concrete knowledge base

Python, LLM-maintained Markdown wiki

A linked literature-review wiki on high-ductility concrete, built from research papers and kept up to date with an LLM following a fixed schema, published as a searchable site.
