About

I came to concrete from the ground up. My undergraduate research used finite element models and neural networks to judge how well cement deep mixing (CDM) improves soft soil, and I worked on site as a supervisor, designer and construction method engineer.

Now, at the Construction Materials Lab of Chonnam National University, I build machine-learning models that predict the compressive strength and tensile strain capacity of fiber-reinforced cementitious composites from their mix design, and turn them into tools the lab can use.
