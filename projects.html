Construction Materials Lab: mix design and strength prediction

JavaScriptPythonMachine learningECC / UHPC / geopolymer

A browser tool for fiber-reinforced cementitious and geopolymer composites. It turns mix proportions into kg per m³ and per batch with cost, embodied energy and CO₂, and predicts compressive strength and tensile strain capacity from the mix, fibers, curing and test age, with an 80% range and a strength-vs-age table.

HDC Wiki: high-ductility concrete knowledge base

PythonLLMLiterature review

A linked wiki on high-ductility concrete built from research papers. An LLM writes and maintains the pages under a fixed schema, and the wiki is published as a searchable site for the literature review.
