Research interests

Machine learning for cementitious composites

Predicting compressive strength and tensile strain capacity of fiber-reinforced and alkali-activated composites (ECC/SHCC, UHPC, geopolymer) from binder, aggregate, fiber and curing inputs, with honest uncertainty ranges.

Ground improvement with cement deep mixing (CDM)

Finite element analysis in PLAXIS combined with feedforward neural networks to evaluate how CDM columns stabilise soft ground.

Geotechnical construction methods

Deep excavations, retaining walls, bored piles and CDM piles: stability, sequencing and safety in practice.
